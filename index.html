Express Removal price sheet

Wilmington / Cape Fear region. Based on a 7x14 dump trailer with 4 ft sides (about 14.5 yd³ full). Measure your actual trailer and adjust. Priced to beat the franchises and match strong local independents while covering New Hanover landfill costs.

How quoting works

  1. Photo estimate: customer texts photos, we give a range (for example, "1/4 to 1/2 load, $195 to $325").
  2. On-site firm price: we walk the job and give one flat number.
  3. Approve before loading: nothing gets lifted until the customer says yes.
  4. Photo proof: snap the loaded trailer against the fill lines for every job.

How we measure a load

Paint or tape lines inside the trailer walls. Where the pile lands is the load size, and the customer can see it too.

Loads between lines are priced at the in-between tiers (1/8, 1/3, 5/8, 7/8). Level with the top of the sides = full.
